About the clinic

Taking the time to give midlife the care it deserves

Midlife health is complex — and too often dismissed. At Liberty Menopause Care, every consultation begins with a thorough review of your personal history: reproductive, medical, and family. There are no rushed appointments and no one-size-fits-all solutions.

Dr Emma Ward combines a deep clinical grounding in general practice with advanced specialist training in menopause care, hormonal health, and women's wellbeing. She holds membership of the British Menopause Society, the European Menopause and Andropause Society, and the National Association for Premenstrual Syndromes.

About Dr Emma Ward

A GP who chose to specialise

Manchester Medical School graduate · 19 years clinical experience

Dr Emma Ward trained at Manchester Medical School and has worked as a GP for nearly two decades. Over time, she developed a specialist interest in women's hormonal health — particularly the underdiagnosed and undertreated area of menopause — and completed advanced training with the British Menopause Society.

She established Liberty Menopause Care to provide the kind of thorough, unhurried care that midlife women deserve: appointments long enough to take a proper history, treatment plans grounded in current evidence, and a clinical relationship built on trust.

How it works

What to expect from your first appointment

01

Get in touch

Use the contact form or email the clinic to enquire. Dr Ward will usually respond within 24 hours to discuss your needs and arrange a suitable appointment time.

02

Initial consultation

A dedicated one-hour appointment — face-to-face in Hayfield or online. Dr Ward takes a thorough medical, reproductive, and family history to understand the full picture.

03

Your treatment plan

Following your consultation, you will receive a written summary and personalised plan covering any recommended investigations, treatments, and follow-up.

04

Ongoing support

Follow-up appointments to review your progress, adjust prescriptions, and answer any questions. Dr Ward provides continuity of care throughout your journey.
